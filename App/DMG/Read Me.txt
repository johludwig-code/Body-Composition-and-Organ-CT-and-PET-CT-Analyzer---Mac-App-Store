Body Composition and Organ CT and PET-CT Analyzer (BCO Analyzer)

FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.
Not a medical device; not for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Research software for the automated segmentation and quantification of
anatomical structures in CT data. Not intended for diagnosis, treatment
planning, or any other clinical decision-making.

Every segmentation and every number is a research measurement that a
qualified person must check before it is used. Results do not belong in a
patient's record. Ethics approval, data protection and the choice of data are
the responsibility of the study's investigators.

Installing
  Drag the app onto the Applications folder. Requires a Mac with Apple
  Silicon, macOS 14 or later, and 16 GB of memory or more.
  A build that is not notarised by Apple: if macOS says it cannot check the
  app, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway once.

Privacy
  The app has no network access. Images, results and identifiers stay on
  this Mac; nothing is uploaded, and no usage data is collected. Keep project
  folders on a FileVault-encrypted disk.
